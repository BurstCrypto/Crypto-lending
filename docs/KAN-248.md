# KAN-248: Logging data governance and incident-query controls

KAN-248 now has a rigorous local draft packet for actor/correlation
classification, least-privilege access, environment separation, administrative
audit, break glass, retention/deletion/legal hold, incident queries, evidence,
ownership, review cadence, and exceptions. This work is deliberately local-only
and is not an approval.

KAN-220 is still `TO_DO`. Security, Privacy, and Operations have not approved
the proposal. The packet therefore remains
`DRAFT_PENDING_EXTERNAL_APPROVAL` / `NOT_EFFECTIVE`, and its validator rejects
any local attempt to claim approval or attach decision evidence.

- [`security/logging-governance-decision.json`](security/logging-governance-decision.json)
  is the canonical version `0.1.0-draft` machine record.
- [`security/logging-governance-decision.sha256`](security/logging-governance-decision.sha256)
  binds the exact packet bytes for drift detection.
- [`security/logging-governance-logger-contract.sha256`](security/logging-governance-logger-contract.sha256)
  binds the exact `logging-context.ts` and `structured-logger.ts` bytes with
  path/length domain separation.
- [`security/logging-governance-decision.md`](security/logging-governance-decision.md)
  is the human decision view.
- [`security/logging-incident-query-runbook.md`](security/logging-incident-query-runbook.md)
  is the provider-neutral incident procedure.

The dependency-free validator rejects stale fingerprints; new/removed context,
event, safe-field, or event-field allowlists; retention-template drift; missing
correlation classification; broadened access; destructive break glass;
unbounded queries; default actor/financial projections; weakened immutable
record language; unknown schema fields; missing repository evidence; and any
fabricated approval state.

The sidecars are integrity evidence, not approvals. A future effective decision
must bind the exact merged commit SHA, tree SHA, packet SHA-256, and logger
contract SHA-256. A branch hash changes when merged and cannot be pre-approved.

## Reconciliation with existing controls

- KAN-49 classifies structured logs and correlation identifiers as
  `RESTRICTED`; KAN-248 keeps that classification for the full record and every
  linkage identifier. It does not change the KAN-49 register or satisfy
  KAN-235's independent threat-model approval.
- KAN-50's write-only workload log capabilities and no-secret/no-raw-payload
  boundary remain prerequisites. The reader, administrator, audit, and
  break-glass proposal does not claim those roles exist.
- KAN-51 remains the emission authority. The validator freezes its actual
  server-root context keys, closed event names, safe fields, per-event fields,
  trace exclusion, and 4,096-byte structured-log implementation through the
  logger source fingerprint. KAN-248 does not approve unfinished web error
  paths, legacy enrichment, or intent/quote producers.
- KAN-52 may link bounded application spans but may not put actor or financial
  identifiers into metric dimensions or the default trace projection. Its
  backend/cost/deployed evidence remains KAN-250 scope.
- The immutable ledger ADR and KAN-41/KAN-42/KAN-43 remain authoritative for
  actor, journal, posting, lifecycle, reversal, and compensation records. Logs
  cannot replace or mutate them.

## Exact pending gates

1. KAN-220 completes with reviewed prerequisite output, and its exact effect is
   reconciled into a new packet version.
2. Security decides actor/correlation classification, access capabilities,
   administrative audit, query use cases, evidence escalation, and break glass.
3. Privacy decides purpose limitation, actor lookup, data-subject handling,
   evidence minimization, deletion, retention, and legal-hold interaction.
4. Operations confirms implementable environment-separated roles, identity
   lifecycle, audit capture/failure behavior, bounded query templates,
   retention/deletion, break-glass activation, automatic revocation, and cost
   ceilings.
5. Legal, Records, and Finance confirm hold authority, evidence schedule,
   disposition, and the immutable financial-record separation.
6. Independent decisions bind the exact merged commit, tree, packet fingerprint,
   and logger-contract fingerprint; all conditions/exceptions have owners and
   expiry.
7. A separately authorized non-production exercise proves delivery, KMS
   encryption, positive/negative access, environment denial, query audit,
   actor-purpose enforcement, expiry, deletion, evidence handling, hold, and
   break-glass/revocation behavior.
8. Remaining KAN-51 web/error-path, legacy rollout/enrichment, and real
   intent/quote producer gates, plus KAN-250 backend/cost/deployed observability
   gates, stay open.

Until those gates pass, KAN-248 must not be represented as approved, effective,
deployed, or operationally accepted.
