## What local evidence can prove

Repository validation can prove that the reviewed template declares separate
API, web, and worker log groups, KMS bindings, a closed retention allowlist, and
disabled-by-default cost-sensitive infrastructure. Unit and end-to-end tests can
prove structured serialization, correlation propagation, source-policy checks,
and absence of synthetic prohibited-data canaries from captured local output.

Local validation cannot prove:

- that a deployed task delivered a record to the intended log group;
- the effective KMS key or resource policy in an account;
- service-side retention or eventual deletion;
- IAM allow and explicit-deny behavior;
- environment separation, break-glass audit/revocation, or query audit trails;
- a deployed API-to-worker trace;
- actual ingestion, storage, query, export, retained-resource, or cleanup cost.

No screenshot, static template, local emulator result, or application
`awaiting-dead-letter` event may be relabeled as that evidence.

## Authorization packet required before execution

The run owner must record all of the following before the first cloud/API call:

1. Approved KAN-248 policy revision and digest, including KAN-220-consistent
   retention/deletion/legal-hold decisions.
2. Exact Git SHA, source tree, API/worker image digests, infrastructure template
   digest, task revisions, account alias, Region, environment, log groups, KMS
   alias, and configuration digest.
3. Named evidence owner, Security/Privacy/Operations witnesses, access
   administrator, billing monitor, cleanup owner, and independent final verifier.
4. Exact allowed actions, principals, start/end time, rollback trigger, evidence
   location, access/retention for evidence, and prohibition on customer data.
5. Cost ceiling covering ingestion, storage through the deletion observation,
   queries, exports, KMS/API activity, retained resources, taxes/overages, and a
   final delayed-billing check. Define automatic/manual stop thresholds.
6. Synthetic trace and failure/canary plan using unique fake values only. The
   real values stay in a restricted execution record and never in Jira/logs.
7. Approved cleanup and abort plan that does not delete evidence needed for a
   security finding or immutable financial record.

An existing environment, free tier, previously approved role, or parent ticket
does not implicitly satisfy these gates.

## Authorized execution sequence

This sequence is planning only; it was not run.

1. Revalidate the exact local revision and all approvals immediately before the
   window. Abort on an expired approval, digest drift, unresolved high/critical
   finding, or unknown retained cost.
2. Establish evidence capture in the approved restricted location. Record UTC
   time synchronization and hashes; never capture raw credentials, policies,
   payloads, or broad logs.
3. Deploy only the exact reviewed artifacts through the separately authorized
   KAN-234 procedure. Record actual task/image/template identities.
4. Generate one uniquely identified synthetic request. Follow its
   server-generated root through API, domain/ledger, outbox, worker, and ledger
   event. Each ID must match its authoritative synthetic record.
5. Run the approved minimum-filter query: one environment, smallest time window,
   required service/event set, and exact server root. Record query/evidence
   hashes and counts, not an unrelated log export.
6. Exercise synthetic private-key, token, credential, signature, and provider
   error canaries. Search for absence without recording their values in the
   general evidence packet.
7. Verify effective delivery, KMS binding, retention, environment separation,
   authorized read, explicit unauthorized read denial, mutation/deletion denial,
   and access auditability.
8. Exercise break glass only if it is separately authorized: approve, activate,
   query minimally, expire/revoke, verify denial, and complete post-use review.
9. Preserve a dedicated synthetic record for the full approved retention period
   and prove eventual deletion after it elapses. A configuration value is not
   deletion evidence.
10. Record findings with severity, owner, due date, disposition, and independent
    retest evidence. Abort acceptance for unresolved high/critical findings.
11. Execute cleanup, revoke temporary access, account for retained resources and
    evidence, record observed/estimated remaining cost, and schedule the final
    billing recheck.
12. An independent verifier records `ACCEPTED`, `CONDITIONAL`, or `REJECTED` only
    after all required evidence and cleanup are complete.

## Query and evidence minimization

- Start from the incident/use-case ID and approved environment, then use the
  narrowest UTC range and exact server-generated correlation root.
- Project only fields approved by KAN-248. Do not request `@message`, raw request,
  payload, headers, actor/contact fields, or wildcard field sets.
- Search one service/event family at a time unless cross-service correlation is
  the authorized purpose.
- Store query text hash, UTC range, result count, operator alias, case/ticket,
  evidence hash, and retention label. Avoid raw result copies when a derived
  assertion is sufficient.
- Stop and escalate unexpected personal/prohibited data; do not broaden the
  query to investigate informally.

Immutable ledger/audit records remain the authoritative financial history.
Application log deletion or legal hold must not mutate those records, and log
retention must not be extended as a substitute for the financial-record policy.

## Stop and rollback conditions

Immediately stop synthetic traffic and further queries on revision drift,
unexpected spend, missing KMS binding, wrong log group/environment, prohibited
data, cross-environment access, unexplained denial bypass, missing audit event,
or loss of cleanup authority. Preserve the minimum evidence needed for the
finding, revoke temporary/break-glass access, and follow the KAN-234 rollback.
Do not delete or overwrite log evidence to make a check pass.
