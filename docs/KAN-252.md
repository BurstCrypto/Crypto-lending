# KAN-252: stablecoin valuation authorization and validation

KAN-252 is the external approval and live-validation gate for the local KAN-66
proposal. This branch starts the ticket with an inert evidence template and
future runbook only. It did not visit a provider site, create an account or
trial, accept terms, select a plan, add payment, create a credential or
endpoint, resolve a provider hostname, read live market data, enable egress,
deploy, invoke hosted CI, or spend money.

The evidence template is
[`docs/valuation/kan-252-valuation-acceptance-template.md`](valuation/kan-252-valuation-acceptance-template.md).
Every live field remains `NOT_AUTHORIZED` / `NOT_RUN`.

## Parent decision binding

KAN-66 proposes Pyth Core signed price feeds as the primary USD source and
Ethereum mainnet Chainlink Data Feeds as fallback and cross-check for the exact
KAN-61 USDC, USDT, and PYUSD assets. It proposes conservative integer valuation,
strict freshness and confidence limits, fail-closed disagreement behavior, a
durable depeg latch, and authenticated manual Risk clearance.

## Separate authorities required

- Risk approves thresholds, conservative value semantics, confidence classes,
  depeg/repeg behavior, unavailable/conflict behavior, durable latching,
  authenticated manual clear/override, incident authority, and financial use.
- Finance/Accounting approves valuation and reconciliation consequences, exact
  plans, quotas, unit/overage/tax exposure, alerts, warning/stop/absolute spend
  ceilings, billing ownership, cleanup tail, and final cost review.
- Data/Architecture approves exact products, feed IDs, proxy/aggregator
  addresses and lifecycle, decimals, timestamps, freshness, cadence,
  aggregation, sequence/update identity, checkpoints, fallback semantics, and
  source independence for all three assets.
- Security approves exact hosts and RPC dependencies, TLS/DNS and redirect
  controls, credential custody/rotation/revocation, least egress, redaction,
  access, kill switches, and incident response.
- Privacy/Legal approves exact entities and contract versions, permitted use,
  redistribution/audit restrictions, DPA, subprocessors, residency, retention,
  deletion, termination, and evidence handling.
- Availability/Operations approves rate limits, latency/availability targets,
  retry/backoff, monitoring/escalation, fallback/failback, recovery, and
  teardown.
- KAN-231 separately approves the exact API/RPC destinations, caller, protocol,
  methods, environment, TLS/DNS controls, cost-control binding, and kill switch.
  KAN-252 cannot change `NO_EXTERNAL_EGRESS`; KAN-231 cannot approve provider
  terms, price semantics, Risk policy, or financial use.
- A named environment authority separately approves account/trial/credential
  creation, bounded non-production traffic, UTC exercise window, and cost caps.

No approval implies another. Approvals must record reviewer identity, evidence,
conditions, conflicts, effective and expiry times, and revocation path.

## Policy that requires explicit approval

The proposed policy treats observations as current through 60 seconds and stale
through 300 seconds; stale values cannot support valuation. It caps Pyth
confidence at 50 bps, calls two-source divergence through 25 bps corroborated,
allows only reporting-level low confidence through 50 bps, and freezes above 50
bps. It classifies downside through 50 bps as normal, above 50 and below 200 bps
as watch, and at least 200 bps as depegged.

A latched asset cannot automatically repeg. Candidate recovery needs four
distinct, strictly ordered, corroborated observations spanning at least 1,800
seconds, each within 25 bps downside and Pyth confidence, followed by a distinct
authenticated Risk clear bound to the exact durable latch. Even that candidate
clear does not itself authorize financial use. Risk and Finance must decide each
rule explicitly; this branch makes no such decision.

## Fail-closed assertions

- An unknown asset, registry mismatch, unapproved feed/contract/host, malformed
  value, missing/invalid timestamp, future/regressing update, replay, stale data,
  unusable confidence, or unavailable source cannot create financial value.
- A source conflict above 50 bps yields an unavailable/null valuation, preserves
  both observations, freezes financial increase, and escalates. It never chooses
  the more favorable source.
- One current source or soft disagreement can support only the explicitly
  approved reporting path; it cannot increase buying power, exposure, credit,
  settlement, or another irreversible state.
- The conservative rate is the minimum eligible lower bound capped at USD 1;
  nominal peg, issuer redemption, and a missing confidence interval are never
  invented.
- A depeg latch is exact-asset, durable, unique, atomic, restart-safe, and cannot
  clear automatically. A manual override cannot rewrite historical evidence or
  substitute a price.
- Retries are bounded and idempotent. Provider recovery requires reconciliation
  before failback; provider status pages are not price or freshness evidence.
- Live traffic is limited to identity and naturally occurring observations.
  Exact threshold and failure evidence comes from the bound local harness or an
  authenticated capture replay, never oracle manipulation, quota exhaustion,
  request flooding, or waiting for a market event.
- Secrets, raw endpoints, signed updates, provider payloads/errors, account data,
  and customer identifiers do not enter broad logs, metrics, or evidence.

## Stop conditions

Stop all requests and disable the approved egress path on unexpected spend,
unknown endpoint or redirect, identity/registry mismatch, unapproved feed or
contract upgrade, credential/payload leakage, missing audit, unbounded retry,
timestamp/sequence ambiguity, unexplained source divergence, correlated failure
outside the approved model, uncertain financial state, inability to latch or
clear atomically, residency/retention drift, or loss of cleanup authority.
Preserve minimum finding evidence; do not generate extra provider traffic,
exhaust quota, influence a publisher, or wait for a market event merely to force
a passing result.
