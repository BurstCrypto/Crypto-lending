# KAN-251: RPC/indexing provider authorization and validation

KAN-251 is the external approval and live-validation gate for the local KAN-62
proposal. This branch includes the inert evidence record and runbook plus a
dormant, synthetic-tested Aave Ethereum transcript executor. The executor is
not registered in the runtime and has no provider endpoint or credential. This
work did not visit a provider site, create an account or trial, select a plan,
add payment, create an endpoint/key, resolve an endpoint, send an RPC request,
enable egress, deploy, invoke hosted CI, or spend money.

The evidence template is
[`docs/rpc-indexing/kan-251-provider-acceptance-template.md`](rpc-indexing/kan-251-provider-acceptance-template.md).
Every live field remains `NOT_AUTHORIZED` / `NOT_RUN`.

## Parent decision binding

KAN-62 proposes Alchemy as primary and QuickNode as an independent fallback for
provider-neutral, standard HTTPS JSON-RPC reads, WSS subscriptions, and
historical backfill. Managed data APIs, add-ons, proprietary namespaces,
transaction broadcast, signing, and automatic resubmission remain excluded.

## Separate authorities required

- Architecture approves the standard-only capability profiles, identity,
  finality, continuity, fallback, and fail-closed semantics.
- Security approves exact HTTPS/WSS hosts inside the access-controlled KAN-231
  record, plus TLS/DNS, credential custody and rotation, redaction, incident
  handling, and least privilege. KAN-251 stores only safe endpoint aliases and
  controlled-record references/digests.
- Privacy/Legal approves exact entities/contracts, DPA, subprocessors,
  retention/deletion/residency, permitted use, and termination.
- Availability/SRE approves SLA/support/escalation, archive/history boundaries,
  retry/backoff, WSS recovery, monitoring, failover/failback, and kill switches.
- Finance approves exact plans, quotas, unit pricing, overages, taxes, monthly
  warning/stop/absolute ceilings, cleanup tail, and billing ownership.
- KAN-231 separately approves exact-host egress and retains the exact-host
  evidence in its controlled record. KAN-251 cannot change
  `NO_EXTERNAL_EGRESS`, and KAN-231 cannot approve provider semantics or terms.
- A named environment authority separately approves account/trial/key creation,
  non-production traffic, and the UTC exercise window.

No role's approval implies another. Reviewers must record conflicts, expiry,
exceptions, and independent final verification.

## Authorized run sequence

This is a future sequence and was not executed.

1. Freeze the exact parent decision/configuration and record all digests,
   approvals, environment, cost ceilings, stop thresholds, owners, and cleanup.
2. Revalidate KAN-62 locally. Abort on digest drift, expired research/terms,

3. Under separate authorization, create only the approved test accounts/plans,

5. Verify exact chain identity before accepting any other response on each of
   the eight provider/network combinations.
6. Exercise every required standard EVM/Solana HTTPS method and WSS
   subscription. Confirm proprietary, debug/trace, managed-add-on, and write
   methods remain unavailable to the adapter.
7. Measure archive start/failure boundaries, bounded latency/throughput/cost,
   quota/rate-limit/429 behavior, retry/backoff/jitter, and response-size limits.
8. Disconnect WSS, prove indexing stops, reconnect with bounded backoff, perform
   HTTPS gap backfill, validate hash/parent continuity, then resume.
9. Exercise stale/unavailable data, reorg/parent divergence, finality stalls,
   identity mismatch, and unsupported capability. Prove no financial credit or
   irreversible state advances.
10. Create controlled primary/fallback disagreement and outage cases. Require
    identity/parity checks, bounded reads, explicit ambiguity, recovery to a
    common ancestor, and safe failback. Never send, sign, broadcast, or
    automatically resubmit a transaction.
11. Verify credential/provider error redaction, access/retention/residency,
    monitoring/escalation, kill switches, and exact observed cost.
12. Stop traffic; revoke/rotate credentials; delete or explicitly retain
    accounts, trials, plans, endpoints, evidence, and egress; capture provider
    deletion state, unbilled/overage estimates, retained costs, and final billing
    recheck.
13. Record findings and independent retest. A verifier independent of provider
    selection, implementation, evidence collection, and finding ownership
    records `ACCEPT`, `CONDITIONAL`, or `REJECT`.

## Fail-closed assertions

- Chain identity is checked before data enters canonical state.
- WSS is a low-latency hint; an uninterrupted, reconciled HTTPS path is required
  before advancing after a gap or provider switch.
- Stale, mismatched, reorged, unavailable, or divergent observations cannot
  create financial credit, settlement, or another irreversible transition.
- Unknown ancestry/history or provider disagreement becomes an explicit blocked
  state with operator escalation.
- Retries are bounded and limited to idempotent reads. There is no automatic
  transaction resend, replacement, or failover broadcast.
- Provider status pages are hints, not chain/finality oracles.
- Raw endpoints, credentials, payloads, errors, signatures, wallet/transaction
  identifiers, and customer data do not enter logs, metrics, or broad evidence.

## Stop conditions

Stop all requests and disable the approved egress path on unexpected spend,
unknown endpoint/redirect, identity mismatch, prohibited method, credential or
payload leakage, unbounded retry, uncertain financial state, continuity gap,
unexplained provider disagreement, cross-region/residency drift, missing access
audit, or loss of cleanup authority. Preserve minimum finding evidence; do not
send extra traffic merely to force a passing result.
